## 👨‍💻 About Me

I am a **Computer Engineering graduate** with professional experience in **data analysis and reporting since 2017**, currently working in Human Resources planning within the Portuguese National Republican Guard (GNR).

I consolidate, validate and transform data from sources such as **IBM AS/400 and Excel**, developing Power BI reports and dimensional data models to monitor workforce levels, analyse trends and support management decisions.

My work combines **Business Intelligence, Data Analytics and People Analytics**, with a particular focus on data quality, consistency, confidentiality and actionable insights.

I am also developing my skills in **SQL and Microsoft Fabric**, exploring modern data integration and transformation workflows, including Bronze, Silver and Gold architecture.
