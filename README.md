## 👨‍💻 About Me

I am a **Data Analyst** and **Computer Engineering graduate**, currently working with **Human Resources data and reporting** within the Portuguese National Republican Guard (GNR).

My practical experience is centred on **Power BI, DAX, Power Query and Excel**, consolidating, transforming and validating institutional data to build analytical models, KPIs and dashboards for operational and management decision support.

I care about **data quality, consistency, traceability and clear communication**. My academic background and continued learning also include SQL, Python/pandas, Snowflake, Microsoft Fabric, Lakehouse and ETL/ELT fundamentals.
